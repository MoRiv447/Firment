## New mechanical gates this round, and what each can catch

1. `crates/firment-tui/tests/detail_fixtures_match_the_producer.rs` — a hand-built `AgentEvent::ToolEnd`
   or `Item::Tool` may not carry `detail` for a tool the producer never fills it for.
2. `the_action_counts_findings_in_the_shape_the_renderer_emits` (`crates/firment-cli/src/main.rs`) —
   the action's grep patterns are checked against real `to_markdown()` output, and the old pattern
   is asserted to match nothing in it.
3. `tokens.test.ts` — 11 tabulated ratio pairs, their hexes and the announced key count scraped out
   of `docs/design/tokens.md`; the `N.NN:1 on|vs <ground>` claims in `tokens.css`'s comments
   recomputed from the palette.
4. `no_site_spawns_probe_rs_outside_the_leased_functions` (`firment-tools`) — a `Command::new` of the
   probe that is not a `--version` presence check is refused; ≥3 leased spawn sites must remain findable.
5. `a_command_that_rewrites_a_transcript_guards_itself_or_says_why` (`gui/src-tauri`) — every
   `#[tauri::command]` that saves a transcript calls `ensure_not_running` or appears in `EXEMPT`
   with a reason.
6. `every_shipped_powershell_script_carries_a_utf8_bom` — walks the repository for `*.ps1`.
7. `a_rename_and_an_edit_of_the_same_file_never_run_in_one_wave`, `step_verdict`'s paired
   build-vs-run case, `the_readme_command_block_only_names_commands_that_exist` (asks
   `Cli::command()`, not a second list), and the two source gates over `App.tsx`/`HardwarePane.tsx`.

Per the round's exit criterion: each of these reads at least one file it does not also write, and
each carries a negative control or a scraped-input floor.

## Not mechanically checkable, and said so rather than gated

- Whether a *new* `#[tauri::command]` needs the running-turn guard at all — the gate proves every
  exemption is stated, not that the statement is right.
- A ratio written as prose instead of a `<ratio>:1 on|vs <ground>` pair with a resolvable ground
  (`--scroll-thumb`'s bare `2.74:1`, the hypothetical fills at 1.43 and 4.37).
- Behavioural claims in description prose ("`firm update` re-installs the running binary"): the
  command *form* is gated, the sentence describing it is not.
- Deleting dead code cannot be proven by a failing test — that is what dead means; Stage 2's
  fail-before is structural and stated that way in its commit message.

## Accepted and deliberate, so the next audit does not re-report them

- `web/src/styles/tokens.css` carries a different palette (`--bg: #0f0f12`) while
  `docs/design/tokens.md` presents itself as the source of truth for three surfaces. `web/` is out
  of scope by the standing decision; the doc's sentence is now scoped to the GUI.
- `dist/SHA256SUMS` written with CRLF by `release/pack.ps1`: `install.sh` matches the sums row by
  field, so a hand-built `dist/` used as a mirror needs the CR stripped. Changing the generator
  would alter the byte form of any existing mirror's sums file, so only the BOM half of that
  finding was fixed.
- `ProbeLease` is per call, not per session: a `debug` session is a run of separate probe-rs calls
  and can lose the probe between two of them. Documented at `tools/util.rs`'s lease doc.
- `Config::save` rewrites `config.toml` without preserving comments; `firm update` is a local
  re-install, not a fetch (README corrected to say so).
- Merging `ci.yml`'s src-tauri gate onto Linux cannot be proven locally: the rehearsal ran on
  Windows, and the job runs on ubuntu with the webkit packages the step installs.
